MindBloom is an application designed to let users organize the books they read, save quotes, and manage personal notes. MindBloom is built on the principle of data minimization: it does not require account creation and does not collect any personally identifiable information.

1. Data We Do NOT Collect

MindBloom does not collect, request, or store:

  • Name, email, or other identifying data
  • Contact information
  • Location information
  • Health data
  • Financial data
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers

The app does not require account creation and does not link any information to your identity.

2. Your Personal Library

All content you enter — books, notes, quotes, ratings, settings, and reading progress — is stored exclusively on your device. This data is never sent to us or to third parties. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data (unless you have created a backup, see point 5).

3. Book Search and External Services

When you use the search feature to add a book (by title, author, or ISBN), MindBloom sends the search term to the following services to retrieve information about the book (cover, author, description, publisher):

  • Google Books API
  • Open Library

To speed up searches and reduce load on these services, results for the most common searches may be temporarily cached on our Supabase server (infrastructure hosted in the European Union, Ireland), for up to 30 days.

These searches contain only the typed text (e.g. the book title) and are in no way linked to your identity: we do not send your name, email, device identifiers, or other personal data along with the search.

4. Device Permissions

The app may request the following permissions, only for related features:

  • Camera: to take a photo to use as a book cover
  • Photo Library: to choose or save cover images
  • Notifications: for reading reminders and daily quotes (managed locally on the device)

You can revoke these permissions at any time from iOS Settings, without this preventing use of the app's core features.

5. Data Backup

MindBloom lets you export a backup of your library to a file that remains under your control (e.g. saved in your local files or iCloud Drive, as you choose). We do not keep copies of your backups on our servers.

6. Third-Party Services

MindBloom does not use analytics, advertising, or user-behavior tracking tools. The only external services contacted are those listed in point 3, solely to provide book search results.

7. Security

Communications with external services take place over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Since we do not collect personal data or user accounts, the risk to your privacy is minimal even in the event of server-side security issues.

8. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR and similar regulations, you can at any time: revoke the iOS permissions granted to the app, delete all your data by uninstalling the app, and export your data via the backup feature before doing so.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated in the future, for example in the event of new features. The most recent version will always be available at this address.
